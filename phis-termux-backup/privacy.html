Privacy Policy

This policy explains the privacy boundaries of the owner-operated PHIS Termux Backup pilot and its informational website.

Google authorization

The utility requests Google's drive.file scope. This limits access to files and folders created by the app or explicitly authorized for it rather than granting access to all unrelated Drive content. Google sign-in and OAuth authorization occur on the owner's device. This website has no OAuth flow and does not collect tokens.

Google data used

During the pilot, the utility uses or is expected to use the authenticated Google account and contents and metadata of app-created or explicitly authorized Drive backup items. Authorized operations may include uploading, listing, verifying, restoring, and deleting backup archives. It is not designed to read unrelated Google account data.

Backup encryption and credentials

Database plaintext is handled on the owner-controlled device and encrypted there with an age public key before upload. Google Drive receives encrypted archives. The recovery key or private identity is not included in the uploaded ciphertext. OAuth credentials, private keys, and other private credentials remain on the owner-controlled device or in the owner's password manager.

Website data practices

This informational website has no custom cookies, login, forms, analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising. It does not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. GitHub may process hosting logs and Google may process authentication and storage data under their own policies. Google Drive holds the encrypted backups, so use involves a third-party storage provider.

Access and revocation

There are no public-user accounts. The owner can revoke OAuth access through their Google Account security settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself remove existing encrypted files from Drive.

Contact and updates

Questions may be raised through the owner's public GitHub profile. This policy may be updated when pilot behavior changes; the updated date identifies the current version. No legal, regulatory, security, or availability assurance is made.
